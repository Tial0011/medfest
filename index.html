What is MedFest?

Not just an event.
An experience.

MedFest is where music, food, culture and community meet in one unforgettable celebration. It is a living expression of Ondo, made for everyone who wants to feel part of something bigger.

Across one electric day, familiar faces become new connections, local stories take centre stage, and every detail invites you to stay a little longer.
