01What is MedFest?

MedFest is an annual lifestyle and social festival celebrating music, food, culture, creativity, businesses and connection.

02Who can attend?

Everyone! MedFest is open to students, young professionals, entrepreneurs, creatives and anyone looking for a great time.

03When is MedFest V?

MedFest V is happening in December 2026. The exact date will be announced soon.

04Where is it happening?

Ondo City, Ondo State, Nigeria. The exact venue will be announced soon.

05How much are tickets?

Ticket prices will be announced soon. Stay connected with us for early-bird and ticket announcements.
